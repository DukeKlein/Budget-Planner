How to use this

  • 50/30/20 guide: about half of your pay for needs, 30% for wants, 20% for savings. It's a starting point, so adjust it to your life.
  • Pay yourself first: plan your savings amounts before wants, and log them like any other expense when you set the money aside.
  • Log daily: a 30-second entry after each purchase beats trying to remember at month end.
  • Review weekly: if a category turns red early in the month, trim another category now instead of overspending later.
  • Next month: switch the month and your plan is copied over, so you only change what's different.
